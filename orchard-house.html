Orchard House

A tropical home in a one-acre wood.

Orchard House sits in the middle of a one-acre wood in Wayanad, with green views on every side.

Type
Residence
Location
Koleri, Wayanad
Year
2022
Size
2,893 sq ft

Tropical, with a modern touch.

Grey tones, exposed concrete and laterite brick cladding give the house its contrast. The plan follows Vastu and the cardinal directions.

Light and shadow.

Through the day, light and shadow move across the textured walls and keep the house calm for the family.
