Mankasurath

A fashion studio built on soft curves and red accents.

Mankasurath is a fashion design studio in Calicut. Display, fitting, work and storage share one open floor.

Type
Fashion design studio
Location
Calicut
Year
2024
Size
775 sq ft

Open plan, soft edges.

Curved workstations and soft transitions between zones remove harsh corners. A warm beige textured wall sets a calm backdrop for the clothes.

Light on the clothes.

Recessed lights and spotlights around the podium pick out the colour and texture of each garment. Red accents draw the eye to key displays.
