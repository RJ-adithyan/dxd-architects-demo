Noushad Residence

A modern mixed-roof home close to family.

A secluded modern home on a 38.7 cent plot in Tavanur, built next to the client's family house.

Type
Residence
Location
Tavanur, Malappuram
Year
2022
Size
4,305 sq ft

Close to family, with room of its own.

The client, an electrical engineer living abroad, wanted to stay close to his roots without losing private space. The house sits beside the family home with no wall between them.

Open, but private.

The plan feels open at first glance. Plants and design elements quietly hide the private rooms from the shared ones.
