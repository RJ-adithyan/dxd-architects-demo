Dr. Ashoora's Dentistry

A calm clinic in a converted shop.

This multi-specialty dental clinic is a refurbished retail space in the town of Edavannappara.

Type
Dental clinic interior
Location
Edavannappara, Malappuram
Year

Size

Away from the stress of a clinic.

The design steps away from the usual hospital feel. Visually connected rooms make the clinic feel open and clear.

Soft in front, precise behind.

Rounded furniture and ceiling edges keep the reception welcoming. The treatment rooms take straighter lines, suited to precise work.
